| Perpetual alternative | Individually quoted by company size and agreed scope at purchase | From EUR 8,000 once |

Subscriptions run for twelve months and are paid annually in advance, plus applicable taxes. They do not automatically renew. Paid use requires a separately accepted Order; downloads, evaluation, revenue growth or continued unauthorized use do not create an automatic charge.

A perpetual purchase provides indefinite use and internal modification of the specified version within its agreed scope, including private forks. Later revenue growth causes no additional license fee, annual reassessment or compulsory tier change. Future versions, upgrades, additional entities and new types of use outside the purchased scope require a separate agreement.

Standard internal use covers one legal entity at all its own sites, with no user, installation, site, operation, planning-volume or run limit or related charge. Group revenue determines the applicable band. Integration, hosting, customization and support are separately quoted. AI model and provider costs are separate.

## Source and distribution rights

The license permits source inspection, modification, private forks, and limited public source forks and patches under the same license. Each recipient needs its own execution entitlement. Source access and compilation do not give an unlimited right to execute, including through tests.

Public executable releases, container images, third-party products and scheduling services require express distribution or Custom rights, subject to the license's contractor and mandatory-law provisions. Internal changes do not have to be published or contributed. Upstream contributions require separately agreed rights sufficient for the project's source and commercial licensing.

At subscription or evaluation expiry, further execution requires another entitlement. Compliant source collaboration and inactive archival retention may continue. Previously lawfully generated production plans remain usable; evaluation outputs remain restricted.
