## Public license behavior

- Free internal production use is available up to and including EUR 2 million applicable annual revenue. Group revenue and Custom deployment triggers apply.
- Business is EUR 2,148 annually above EUR 2 million and below EUR 10 million. Business Plus is EUR 4,308 annually from EUR 10 million to below EUR 50 million. Revenue from EUR 50 million, multi-entity operational use, SaaS, OEM and White Label require Custom.
- Qualifying non-commercial academic research and teaching are free without a revenue threshold or evaluation limit. Non-operational evaluation is available for 90 days per entity.
- Subscriptions last twelve months, are paid annually in advance and renew only by agreement. A paid entitlement always needs an accepted Order.
- Perpetual licenses are quoted from EUR 8,000 once for the purchased version and scope. Later revenue growth does not create additional fees, annual reassessment or compulsory tier changes.
- Source inspection, modification, compilation, private forks and limited public source collaboration are permitted. Execution, including tests, requires a Free, research, evaluation or paid entitlement. A public fork does not expand execution rights.
- Source and inactive copies may be retained within the license's conditions after execution expires. Existing lawful production outputs remain usable, but new calculations need an entitlement.
- Gratuitous permissions include an as-is disclaimer and qualified defect exclusion. Paid defect remedies are treated separately. Customer-caused defects and losses are allocated by causation; unrelated original defects remain actionable. Ordinary-negligence damages are excluded outside material contractual obligations and otherwise limited to foreseeable loss typical of the agreement, subject to the express exceptions in section 11.4.
- Governing law and jurisdiction follow the relevant legal rules. There is no exclusive forum or particular governing law selected by the public license.
- The Licensor is the copyright holder or authorized grantor making the Software available. Each grant extends only to rights that grantor controls. This definition does not assign ownership to a trading name.

These rules are in the LICENSE itself. Other repository documents do not silently amend them. Standard scope remains one entity at all its own sites with no user, installation, site, operation, planning-volume or run metering. Services and AI provider costs are separate.

## Using the commercial templates

The public license does not require completion of an Order for eligible free use. Paid use needs a concrete agreement, and the templates are preparation material for that agreement.

Before using a commercial or contributor template:

1. Insert the verified legal parties, addresses and contractual contacts; confirm the signer's authority and the grantor's rights in the relevant material.
2. Identify the covered entity or entities and use. For perpetual purchases, identify the version immutably; for subscriptions, enter the start and end dates.
3. Agree the actual price, tax treatment and payment arrangements. Keep the selected thresholds and perpetual protection unless the parties expressly agree a permitted departure.
4. Identify and supply retainable copies of every incorporated document. Complete any additional governing-law, venue or service provisions; remove unused template fields before acceptance.
5. Record express acceptance. Posting a contribution, downloading a release or reaching a revenue threshold does not itself accept a separate paid or contributor agreement.

The GTC and contributor template retain draft status. Their placeholders do not form part of the public LICENSE. Custom rights and separate hosting, integration, support or development services must be granted expressly.

## Publication and legal review scope

The LICENSE is the repository's publication text, not an assertion that counsel has reviewed it or that ownership and third-party dependencies have been audited.

For distribution, retain this license with the source, verify authority over the actual release contents and comply with third-party notices and licenses. Customer data, credentials and generated private artifacts remain outside distribution. Publication cleanup and other release work are described in [publication preparation](../publication.md).

License 1.1 replaces the former general reliance on statutory liability with differentiated exclusions and limits in section 11. It preserves intent, gross negligence, personal injury, fraud, guarantees and mandatory liability, and does not impose a zero-fee or license-fee-only damages cap. The [revision notes](liability-notes.md) explain remaining exposure and the legal review points. Publication of 1.1 does not retrospectively amend rights or Orders already governed by 1.0; an applicable agreement or acceptance is needed for a change affecting those rights.
