# Warranty and liability changes in License 1.1

24 September 2026. Explanatory review notes; not incorporated terms, a legal opinion or confirmation of enforceability.

The operative wording is in [LICENSE section 11](../../LICENSE). Version 1.1 strengthens the protection for the Licensor while keeping free and paid use distinct. It does not change revenue thresholds, prices, source permissions, execution entitlements or perpetual protection. Earlier grants and Orders are not amended merely by changing the repository file.

## What changed

| Topic | License 1.1 |
| --- | --- |
| Additional performance promises | No additional guarantee of uninterrupted or entirely error-free operation, an optimal schedule, compatibility with undisclosed environments or a particular commercial outcome. Agreed functionality and applicable legal requirements still control. |
| Free permissions | As-is/as-available delivery, no additional express or implied warranty, and exclusion of defect claims where legally permissible for the particular gratuitous grant. Paid rights and paid services cannot be relabeled free to obtain the exclusion. |
| Self-hosting and changes | The Licensee is responsible for its infrastructure, safeguards, backups, inputs and modifications. Claims are affected by causation and the contractual scope, not automatically lost because a fork exists. Original defects and work the Licensor agreed to supply remain distinguishable. |
| Paid defect remedies | Agreed functionality and statutory defect rights remain. The Licensor has a reasonable opportunity to investigate and provide a legally sufficient cure. Source availability does not force the buyer to repair defects at its own expense. No statutory claim period is shortened. |
| Ordinary negligence | Liability outside material contractual obligations is excluded. For breach of those obligations, damages are limited to foreseeable loss typical of the agreement. A material obligation is explained in the license, not merely labeled a cardinal duty. |
| Mandatory and express exceptions | Intent, gross negligence, injury to life/body/health, fraud, express guarantees within their scope, mandatory product liability and other non-waivable rights override exclusions. |
| German rental-law initial defects | A targeted clause excludes no-fault damages liability under BGB section 536a(1), first alternative, only if German rental law actually governs the business or institutional software-use agreement. Cure, reduction and fault-based claims are not removed. |
| Separate services | The gratuitous-use disclaimer does not cover paid hosting, support, integration or development. Their own agreements determine those obligations. |

## Why this is not a universal exclusion

Making source available and allowing customer modifications do not by themselves remove the supplier's obligations. A modification matters where it caused the particular defect or loss and was outside the Licensor's contractual responsibility. A paid grant may still carry duties to provide the agreed functionality and remedies for defects.

The gratuitous classification depends on the actual transaction and applicable law. The license does not assume every free download is legally a gift or every subscription is legally a rental agreement. Statutory privileges for genuinely gratuitous grants remain available where they apply, without promising a particular classification.

There is no automatic numerical cap tied to license fees. A zero-fee cap for free users or a cap at a small annual license fee can conflict with duties whose foreseeable consequences are much larger. Foreseeable production loss or lost profit resulting from a material breach is not categorically excluded merely by its name. Consequently, meaningful liability can remain, particularly for core performance failures and the express exceptions.

The as-is language and statutory carve-outs are not an assurance that an otherwise excessive clause will be judicially repaired. Standard-term enforceability, formation, customer status and applicable law need to be assessed for the actual distribution and contracts. The public license continues to make no choice of law or exclusive forum.

## Primary references checked

The following sources inform the changes. They are not incorporated conditions and do not establish that this particular license has been upheld by a court.

- [BGB section 276(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__276.html): advance exclusion of liability for intent is prohibited.
- [BGB section 309 nos. 7 and 8](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__309.html), [section 307](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__307.html) and [section 310](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__310.html): restrictions on standard terms, personal-injury and gross-fault exclusions, defect remedies and the distinct treatment of business transactions. Section 309 is not directly applied to business terms as though they were consumer terms; section 307 remains relevant.
- [BGB section 444](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__444.html) and [section 536d](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__536d.html): limits on relying on defect exclusions in cases including fraudulent concealment and, under section 444, assumed quality guarantees.
- [BGB sections 521](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__521.html), [523](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__523.html) and [524](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__524.html): statutory treatment of gratuitous gifts where that classification applies. These provisions do not establish the classification of every software grant.
- [BGB section 536a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__536a.html): different bases for rental defect damages and self-remedy. License 11.6 targets the initial-defect no-fault basis, not the entire section or other rental remedies.
- [Product Liability Act section 14](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/prodhaftg/__14.html): applicable statutory product liability cannot be excluded in advance.
- [BGH judgment VIII ZR 155/99](https://juris.bundesgerichtshof.de/cgi-bin/rechtsprechung/document.py?Art=pm&Blank=1&Datum=2000&Gericht=bgh&file=dokument.pdf&linked=urt&nr=23535): standard terms must not remove foreseeable, typical damages for breaches of essential contractual obligations merely by excluding categories of consequential loss.

## Focus for professional review

Review the gratuitous-use defect exclusion for the actual recipient groups, the material-obligation limitation in an industrial planning context, the targeted rental-law clause, and interactions with guarantees, consumer rights and any applicable product-liability regime. Confirm the treatment of customer-caused changes without reversing proof rules. Align any separately negotiated services, insurance arrangements and commercial warranties with the scope actually sold.

The changes are deliberately recorded as a new license version. They do not take protection away from an existing customer simply because the public file was updated.
